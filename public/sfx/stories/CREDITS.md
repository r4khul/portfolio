The rewind impact/ring and watch escapement contacts are original synthesis, without borrowed recordings. The watch reveal (`watch-reveal.wav`) also contains an original five-second crime/mystery composition, beginning at 0.60 seconds after the two ticks. A fixed D3 resonant/bowed-like pedal and low A2 body acquire A-flat/F inner strings; the sparse felt-like motif then climbs F4–A-flat4–E-flat5 toward a late, unresolved revelation. A bowed E-flat adds weight against the D pedal without resolving it. This harmonic and motif development gives the phrase a dark, rising dramatic arc rather than extending a single drone. The instruments and composition are synthesized directly by `scripts/generate_story_sfx.py`; no recorded music, third-party melody, noise bed, or pitch-sweep effect is used. The five-second score and complete 5.60-second cue end with a 500ms clean fade.

All generation, trimming, and mixing steps are retained in `scripts/generate_story_sfx.py`; source excerpts, original Kenney OGGs, and their provenance/license notices are retained under `scripts/audio-sources/`.

## Chapter 4 — The Greedy Man

All three Chapter 4 phrases are original synthesis and composition generated by `scripts/generate_greedy_sfx.py`, using only the Python standard library. They contain no sampled recordings, borrowed melody, third-party score, speech, or stock transition effect. The deterministic generator retains every layer, timing, filter and final mastering level, so the WAVs can be regenerated offline without downloads or audio software.

- **`roadside-arrival.wav` (6.00 seconds):** only a quiet synthesized car approaching and stopping. A warm, low engine enters at 0.55 seconds and gently decelerates into a soft idle by 1.80 seconds. Its quiet tail fades from 2.20–2.90 seconds; the remainder is digital silence. Low-mid harmonics carry the small sound on phone/laptop speakers without a boomy rumble. There is no cooking sound, cookware, insect, random noise, traffic hiss, wind-like sweep, mechanical tick, music or spoken clue. The new filename replaces the earlier ambient draft in browser caches.
- **`surveillance-memory.wav` (5.00 seconds):** a sparse original memory phrase for Grahan’s mental comparison of arrival hours. Three warm, damped low-string gestures occupy 0.45–0.69, 1.65–1.94 and 2.85–3.06 seconds. E3 moves briefly to F3 and returns to E3 as arrival hours find a match; a quiet E3/B3 agreement occupies 3.75–4.20 seconds. Smooth attacks and rapidly darkening partials give a muted string body, separated by silence. There is no pencil, paper, mechanical clock tick, interface ping, whoosh, or sustained background drone. The new filename replaces the earlier writing-related draft in browser caches.
- **`opportunity.wav` (5.00 seconds):** an original restrained crime/mystery phrase over a C/G foundation. Three dark upper articulations descend E-flat–D–D-flat at 0.40, 1.70 and 3.30 seconds as recognition becomes a plan. B-flat and a late F-sharp string voice add unresolved pressure. The harmony gathers through 4.20 seconds before a clean 550ms ending fade. It is a different melodic and harmonic development from Chapter 3's rising watch reveal.

The Chapter 4 masters play at the reader's existing unity gain. Their respective peak ceilings are 0.12, 0.30 and 0.38, retaining headroom while keeping atmosphere and music audible alongside the earlier chapters. Browser visibility, mute, reduced-motion and navigation cleanup use the same shared Web Audio controller as Chapter 3.
