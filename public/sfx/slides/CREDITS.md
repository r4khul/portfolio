# Slide memory sound credits

## Game Console & Ice Tubes

`game-console-bed.m4a` and `game-console-bed.ogg` are one original, seamlessly looping music bed for the whole memory. They are original synthesis and composition, generated by `scripts/generate_slides_bed.py` using only the Python standard library, with ffmpeg used only for the final encodes. They contain no sampled recordings, borrowed melody, third-party score, speech or stock effect. The generator uses fixed seeds, so every run renders byte-identical audio.

The bed sits under the words rather than in front of them: calm, nostalgic and loving, with a little childlike play. It is music only. There are no nature sounds, ambience or noise bed, and no drums, vocals, risers or swells, so later scene sounds have room on top.

- **Key and tempo:** F major with a lydian colour, at a nominal 60 BPM in 4/4. Each chord holds for three bars (12 seconds), and the 24-bar progression is the whole loop: Fmaj9, G6/F, Am7, Bbmaj9, Fadd9/A, Dm9, Bbmaj7#11, C9sus4. The C9sus4 resolves into Fmaj9 by step across the loop point.
- **Pad:** a warm, light sustained pad. Four upper voices stay between C4 and E5, over a thin root voice (F3 to C4, dipping to D3 once). Each voice is two softly low-passed additive oscillators detuned by 2–4 cents, the sharper one leaning left and the flatter one leaning right, which gives a gentle chorus-like width. Chords dissolve into one another through seven-second equal-power crossfades, and pitches shared between chords are held rather than re-struck. A tiny level drift (±7%) keeps the voices breathing.
- **Music box:** 23 sparse notes between E5 and E6, in short phrases with long silences, in the F major pentatonic with chord colours (B over G/F, the raised fourth E over Bb). A recurring short-short-long figure carries the childlike idea. Each note has a soft 6 ms onset, a slowly beating twin fundamental like a steel tine, and a brief metallic glint that fades within about 100 ms.
- **Space:** a soft, dark stereo room (2.8-second decay, 18 ms pre-delay) holds the music box and a little of the pad.
- **Low end:** a gentle 2nd-order 170 Hz high-pass (−0.7 dB at C4, −11 dB at 90 Hz) leaves almost no low end or rumble on headphones. Energy below 90 Hz sits about 37 dB under the mix.

**Loop and levels.** The loop is 96.000 seconds. Both files decode to exactly 4,608,000 stereo frames at 48 kHz: the `.m4a` is AAC-LC at 128 kbps, and the `.ogg` is Opus at 88 kbps (constrained VBR). The master peaks at 0.143 (−16.9 dBFS; true peak about −17 dBTP), with an integrated loudness of −28.0 LUFS and a loudness range of 1.4 LU. Short-term (3-second) loudness stays between −28.9 and −26.8 LUFS. By RMS, the music box stays at least 8.5 dB under the pad in every 400 ms window. It is heard because it sits above the pad's register, not because it is louder.

**How it loops.** The bed is rendered circularly at 32 kHz: every held note, music-box ring, filter state and reverb tail that runs past the end is wrapped to the start, and every slow modulation completes a whole number of cycles per loop. The last sample therefore flows into the first with no crossfade, gap or level step. For delivery, three back-to-back copies are resampled 3:2 to 48 kHz and only the middle copy is kept, so the resampler never sees an edge. Each encode also begins with a 200 ms lead-in taken from the end of the loop, which the container tells the decoder to discard (the MP4 edit list for AAC, the Ogg pre-skip for Opus). The encoder has therefore settled before the loop's first sample, and the join is as clean as any other point in the file. The 24-bit master WAV is not checked in.

**Playback note.** Loop the decoded buffer with Web Audio (`AudioBufferSourceNode.loop`); an `<audio loop>` element does not loop gaplessly. Some tools report the `.ogg` container as 96.21 seconds because they count the extended pre-skip; the decoded audio is exactly 96.000 seconds.

Regenerate with `python3 scripts/generate_slides_bed.py` (add `--ffmpeg PATH` if ffmpeg is not on PATH, or `--wav PATH` to keep the master WAV outside the repository).
